## Platform

web

## Users
High-ticket service brands, clinics (Dental/Medical), e-commerce/retail (Bookstores), tourism agencies, car dealerships, restaurants, and e-learning platforms, with a specific focus on the MENA market. They care about scaling revenue, reducing manual effort, and achieving undisputed premium positioning in their market.
## Product Purpose
Fleevo is a digital design and development agency that engineers "Autonomous Profit Engines". The purpose is to replace slow, manual business operations with high-performance digital platforms that act as 24/7 autonomous employees—capturing leads, closing sales, and running operations seamlessly.

## Positioning
Unlike standard web design agencies that build static brochures, Fleevo builds high-conversion digital ecosystems. The core mechanism combines stunning, premium front-end design with battle-tested backend architecture (Next.js, PostgreSQL, Supabase) to guarantee instantaneous loading (zero drop-off) and active revenue generation.

## Operating Context
Clients use Fleevo's systems to handle high-volume frictionless reservations, sync leads directly to CRMs or sales teams via webhooks, and manage scalable e-commerce distribution. The agency itself operates a heavily automated internal "Command Center" dashboard (Trello/Supabase sync) for lead and project delivery.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Must communicate uncompromising technical superiority, extreme precision, and luxury positioning (+ contracts).
- Next.js edge-network architecture.
- Supabase/PostgreSQL backend for CRM and lead routing.

1. **Design for Business Results:** Every animation and element must be engineered to increase conversion rates and drive revenue.
2. **Automate the Grind:** The systems must do the heavy lifting so clients can focus on scaling rather than surviving.
3. **Project Unquestionable Authority:** The visual aesthetic must immediately command respect and justify high-ticket pricing.
